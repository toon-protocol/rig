`@toon-protocol/rig` and the `rig` CLI are deprecated, and this is the last release. Push and pull with a TOON client (`toon_cli` or `toon-client`) and the `git` skill in `toon-protocol/toon-plugins`; browse repositories in the relay's `/repo` UI. Published pointer pages still load but are no longer maintained. See rig#261.
